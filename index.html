NATIONAL CAPACITY

The systems beneath the future.

UAF's longer horizon looks beyond individual programs to the physical and technical systems that shape what the country can build, move, protect, and sustain. Six concept-stage initiatives explore that next layer.

NC–01 · TRANSIT

The Iron Corridor Initiative

A proposed national rail framework, beginning with regional corridor research and qualified public-interest planning.

Explore → NC–02 · INDUSTRIAL CAPACITY

The Keystone Foundry Project

A practical review of one facility or material supply chain: production constraints, quality, energy, workforce, and the next modernization decision.

Explore the proposal → NC–03 · NEIGHBORHOOD MOBILITY

Connected Communities Mobility

Start with one everyday route. Study sidewalks, crossings, bicycle connections, and transit access as one continuous journey.

Explore the proposal → NC–04 · PUBLIC-INTEREST LOGISTICS

Skyway Infrastructure & Logistics

Test the delivery need and the handoff on the ground before considering a qualified, appropriately authorized flight partner.

Explore the proposal → NC–05 · WATER RESILIENCE

Continental Watershed Restoration

Build an evidence-led baseline for one river reach, then compare restoration and maintenance options before buying sensors or starting works.

Explore the proposal → NC–06 · LAND STEWARDSHIP

The Heritage Ecosystems Initiative

Help a land manager decide what to observe, what to maintain, and when a new device would add more burden than value.
