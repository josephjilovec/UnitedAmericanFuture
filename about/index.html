MISSION & GOVERNANCE

Build the institution.
Then build the impact.

The mission is ambitious. The operating standard should be disciplined, nonpartisan, transparent, and measurable.

WHY UAF

A country moves forward when more people can participate in its future.

United American Future is being built as a nonpartisan civic organization centered on practical opportunity, community strength, service, and long-term resilience.

Operating principle

Make fewer promises. Define the work. Publish the evidence.

What we believe

01

Opportunity should be usable.

Education and economic mobility matter when people can actually reach them.

02

Communities know their terrain.

National ambition works best when local context shapes execution.

03

Trust is built in public.

Governance, spending standards, methods, and outcomes should become visible as the organization matures.

GOVERNANCE ROADMAP

Structure before scale.

  1. FormationEstablish legal entity, governing documents, board duties, and conflict standards.
  2. ComplianceComplete required registrations and determine verified tax status before making tax-deductibility claims.
  3. ProgramsApprove initiatives with defined objectives, budgets, delivery owners, and reporting criteria.
  4. Public reportingPublish governance and financial documents as they become available.
