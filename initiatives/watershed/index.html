The vision

A river connects
more than its banks.

Water ties together neighborhoods, farms, habitat, and infrastructure. What happens in one part of a watershed can matter far beyond it. This initiative keeps those wider connections in view.

Its public focus is river restoration, healthy riparian landscapes, and the care of water systems over time. Technology is part of the wider picture, serving the needs of the place.
