The purpose

Bring places closer.
Open more possibilities.

The United American High-Speed Rail Network is a long-horizon vision for connecting major American population centers through regional passenger rail.

The idea brings intercity journeys, regional connections, and the everyday experience of reaching a station into one picture. Its value would lie in what a more connected country makes possible for people and communities.

Iron Corridor is an initiative within United American Future. This page presents its public concept, not an approved infrastructure project.

The United American High-Speed Rail Network

Three potential hubs.
A national horizon.

The concept imagines Denver, Chicago, and Houston as central connections within a wider network. These locations and the illustrated connections are possibilities, not final decisions.

About the concept

A few clear
answers.

Is this an approved high-speed rail project?+

No. It is a UAF concept. The map does not establish land rights, approved routes, station sites, or permission to build.

Are the three hubs and all the connections final?+

No. They illustrate the national vision. No final network, service technology, speed, or construction schedule is announced.

Is UAF operating a railway?+

No. Iron Corridor is a concept-stage initiative, not an operating railway or a ticketed passenger service.
