The vision

Opportunity begins
with being able to get there.

A job, school, appointment, or neighborhood business is more useful when people can reach it. Connected Communities focuses on the everyday links that make a place work for people with different ages, abilities, and ways of traveling.

The whole journey matters: leaving home, crossing the street, reaching a stop, boarding, and getting back again. The concept treats those connections as one experience.

Public value

A connected journey
belongs to everyone.

An imagined trip shows the public purpose of the idea. These are everyday experiences, not a selected route or an engineering plan.

Neighborhood mobility / A closer look

Access is a continuous
experience.

An inviting street and a useful transit service should connect with each other. The concept puts the experience of the whole trip at the center, including people who use mobility aids.

The image is an imagined scene. It is not an accessibility certification or a design for an announced street project.
